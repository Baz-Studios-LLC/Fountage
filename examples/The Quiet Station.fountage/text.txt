Title: The Quiet Station
Author: Baz Studios

Chapter One

The station clock had stopped at four seventeen, but the trains kept arriving. Mara was the only person who seemed to notice.

She folded the timetable and listened. Somewhere beyond the platform, a bird repeated the same three notes.

***

By morning, there were seven clocks on her kitchen table. None of them agreed.
